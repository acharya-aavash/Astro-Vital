🌟 Overview
ASTRO-VITAL is a crew-first astronaut health companion designed to help space crews monitor, understand, and act on their health during long-duration space missions. Operating far from Earth where astronauts serve as their own first responders, the platform bridges the operational gap by making health information clear, actionable, and medically safe.   

🎯 Key Features & Capabilities
Comprehensive Health Monitoring

Tracks five core health areas: Immunity, Bones & Muscles, Heart & Circulation, Mind & Mood, and Sleep & Energy.   


Separates self-reported symptoms from measured values with built-in data quality checks and measurement instructions.   


Personalized Understanding & Trends

Compares new entries against personal baselines and recent trends to spot changes early.   


Uses clear status categories ranging from Nominal and Watch to Caution and Critical.   


Safe Guidance & Clinical Escalation

Provides actionable next steps and guidance without diagnosing or altering medications.   


Routes critical concerns and symptoms to flight surgeons for professional medical review.   


Crew Engagement & Healthy Routines

Encourages participation through daily quests, XP systems, and streak tracking.

Follows strict guardrails to protect health data privacy and avoid rewarding overtraining or risky behaviors.



🛰️ Addressed Spaceflight Hazards
Long-duration space missions introduce unique physiological and psychological stressors that ASTRO-VITAL addresses:

Microgravity: Mitigating risks related to bone density loss, muscle wasting, and cardiovascular adaptation.   


Radiation: Monitoring persistent exposure concerns during spaceflight.   


Isolation: Supporting behavioral health, mood stabilization, and sleep hygiene.   


Closed Habitat: Managing shared cabin air, limited resources, and constrained daily routines.
