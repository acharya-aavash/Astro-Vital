# 🚀 ASTRO-VITAL
**Your health. Your mission. Your future.**  
*Presented by Team Xenon (Hardik Adhikari & Aavash Acharya)*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**ASTRO-VITAL** is a crew-first astronaut health companion designed to help space crews monitor, understand, and act on their health during long-duration space missions. Operating far from Earth where astronauts serve as their own first responders, the platform bridges the operational gap by making health information clear, actionable, and medically safe.

---

## 🎯 Core Features & Capabilities

1. **Comprehensive Health Monitoring:** Tracks five key health systems (*Immunity*, *Bones & Muscles*, *Heart & Circulation*, *Mind & Mood*, and *Sleep & Energy*) while separating self-reported symptoms from measured values.
2. **Personalized Trends:** Compares check-ins against personal baselines using clear status tiers (*Nominal*, *Watch*, *Caution*, *Critical*).
3. **Safe Guidance & Escalation:** Offers actionable guidance without diagnosing, routing critical issues directly to flight surgeons.
4. **Crew Engagement:** Fosters healthy routines using daily quests, XP systems, and streaks while safeguarding privacy.

---

## 🛰️ Addressed Spaceflight Hazards

1. **Microgravity:** Counters bone density loss, muscle wasting, and cardiovascular shifts.
2. **Radiation:** Tracks persistent space exposure risks.
3. **Isolation:** Supports behavioral health, mood stability, and sleep hygiene.
4. **Closed Habitat:** Helps manage shared cabin air, limited resources, and daily routines.
