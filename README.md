ASTRO-VITAL
Your health. Your mission. Your future.

Introduction
A crew-first health app that empowers astronauts to monitor five core health systems, detect early physiological changes against their personal baselines, and know precisely when to escalate concerns.

Quick Highlights
5 Health Systems Tracked: Comprehensive coverage of Immunity, Bones & Muscles, Heart & Circulation, Mind & Mood, and Sleep & Energy.

Personalized Baselines: Evaluates data against the individual astronaut’s unique baseline rather than generic population averages.

4 Clear Status Levels: Intuitive color-coded indicators ranging from 🟢 Nominal and 🟡 Watch to 🟠 Caution and 🔴 Critical.

Safe Clinical Guidance: Provides clear, actionable next steps without diagnosing, routing high-priority issues straight to the flight surgeon.

Gamified Habits: Built-in daily quests, XP progression, and streaks to sustain consistent wellness routines.

Privacy by Design: Local-first data architecture ensuring complete security and confidentiality.
