🌟 Overview
ASTRO-VITAL is a crew-first astronaut health companion designed to help space crews monitor, understand, and act on their health during long-duration space missions. Operating far from Earth where astronauts serve as their own first responders, the platform bridges the operational gap by making health information clear, actionable, and medically safe.   

🎯 Key Features & Capabilities
* **Core Features & Capabilities**
* **Comprehensive Health Monitoring:** Tracks five key health systems (*Immunity*, *Bones & Muscles*, *Heart & Circulation*, *Mind & Mood*, *Sleep & Energy*) while separating self-reported symptoms from measured values.
* **Personalized Trends:** Compares check-ins against personal baselines using clear status tiers (*Nominal*, *Watch*, *Caution*, *Critical*).
* **Safe Guidance & Escalation:** Offers actionable guidance without diagnosing, routing critical issues directly to flight surgeons.
* **Crew Engagement:** Fosters healthy routines using daily quests, XP systems, and streaks while safeguarding privacy.


* **Addressed Spaceflight Hazards**
* **Microgravity:** Counters bone density loss, muscle wasting, and cardiovascular shifts.
* **Radiation:** Tracks persistent space exposure risks.
* **Isolation:** Supports behavioral health, mood stability, and sleep hygiene.
* **Closed Habitat:** Helps manage shared cabin air, limited resources, and daily routines.


* **References & Data Sources**
